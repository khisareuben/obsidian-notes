## STAGE 1: NOW - YEAR 1.2 (You Are Here)
### Master What TUM Is Already Giving You

**Goal:** Don't just pass DICT units. Turn DICT units into CCNA skills.

### What To Do In Class (This Semester):
1. **CCNA 1 - Introduction to Networks:** Master this 100%. IP addressing, subnetting, OSI, TCP/IP. If you don't master subnetting here, CCNA will be hell later.
2. **Networking Units:** Every time lecturer teaches a topic, ask: "How would I configure this on a Cisco router?" Don't just memorize theory.
3. **Lab Every Day:** TUM has Cisco labs / Packet Tracer. Use them. Don't wait for practical exam.
   - Build a network in Packet Tracer every week
   - Configure 2 routers + 2 switches + 4 PCs
   - Break it, fix it. That is CCNA exam practice.

### What To Do Outside Class (Your Secret Advantage):
- **Packet Tracer (Free from Cisco Networking Academy):** Install it on your laptop. Lab 30-60 mins daily. This is your #1 weapon.
- **Professor Messer / Jeremy's IT Lab (Free on YouTube):** Jeremy's IT Lab is BEST for CCNA. Watch CCNA 200-301 playlist alongside your DICT.
- **Subnetting Practice:** Do 10 subnetting questions per day until you can do one in under 60 seconds. CCNA exam is timed.

**What NOT To Do Now:**
- Don't pay for A+ exam yet. Your DICT covers it.
- Don't pay for Network+ exam yet. Your DICT + CCNA covers it.
- Don't chase 5 certs at once. Focus: DICT + CCNA.

**Milestone To Pass Stage 1:**
Can you do this without Googling?
- Subnet 192.168.1.0/26 into 4 subnets?
- Configure a router with IP, OSPF, and a switch with 2 VLANs in Packet Tracer?
- Explain OSI vs TCP/IP to a Year 1.1 student?

If yes, you are ready for Stage 2. If no, stay here and lab more.

## STAGE 2: YEAR 1.2 - YEAR 2.1 - GET CCNA WHILE IN SCHOOL
### This Is Your Biggest Advantage. Use It.

**Cert:** Cisco CCNA (200-301)
**Cost:** ~$300 (Check if TUM / Cisco Academy gives student discount voucher - Academy students often get 50-70% off. ASK your lecturer / Academy instructor NOW. This alone can save you 15k-20k KES)
**Study Time:** 2 semesters, alongside DICT
**Why Now?** You have free labs, free lecturer help, classmates to study with, and you are already studying CCNA content. After you graduate, you lose all that and have to pay for labs and training yourself.

### CCNA Breakdown - What TUM Teaches vs Exam:
- **CCNA 1:** Networking Fundamentals (You are doing this now)
- **CCNA 2:** Switching, Routing, Wireless Essentials
- **CCNA 3:** Enterprise Networking, Security, Automation

If TUM teaches all 3 modules, you are being prepared for the FULL CCNA exam. Don't waste it.

### Your CCNA Study Plan (Alongside DICT):
**Monday - Friday (In School):**
- Attend all networking / CCNA classes. Don't skip.
- After class: 1 hour Packet Tracer lab on THAT DAY'S topic.

**Weekend:**
- 2 hours: Jeremy's IT Lab CCNA videos + Boson / free practice questions
- 1 hour: Subnetting + Lab revision

**Holiday (Long Holiday After Year 1.2):**
- This is your CCNA bootcamp. 4-6 hours per day for 3-4 weeks. Lab, lab, lab.
- Book CCNA exam at end of holiday or start of Year 2.1. Don't wait until you "feel ready" - you will never feel 100% ready.

### Lab Checklist Before You Book CCNA Exam:
- [ ] Can configure VLANs, trunking, STP on switch
- [ ] Can configure OSPF, static routing, NAT, ACLs on router
- [ ] Can configure DHCP, DNS, wireless on router
- [ ] Can troubleshoot: "PC1 can't ping PC2" - find fault in 10 mins in Packet Tracer
- [ ] Can do subnetting in under 60 seconds
- [ ] Scored 85%+ on 2 full practice exams

**Job After CCNA:** You are now employable as Network Support / Junior Network Engineer, even before you finish DICT. This is rare. Most DICT graduates have NO cert. You will have DICT + CCNA.

## STAGE 3: YEAR 2.1 - YEAR 2.2 - ATTACHMENT / INTERNSHIP
### Turn CCNA Into Real Experience

**Goal:** Get 3 months attachment that gives you REAL network experience, not just photocopying.

**Where To Apply In Mombasa (Target These):**
- ISPs: Safaricom, Airtel, Liquid Telecom, Seacom, Mawingu, Poa Internet
- Banks / SACCOs: IT department
- TUM ICT Department itself (ask!)
- Hotels / Hospitals with large networks
- Cyber / IT companies: Computer repair + networking shops that do installations

**What To Say When Applying:**
> "I am a DICT student at TUM, Year 2, and I am CCNA certified (or CCNA in progress). I can configure Cisco routers, switches, VLANs, and troubleshoot networks. I am looking for attachment where I can work on real networks, not just help desk."

That sentence will put you ahead of 90% of DICT students who apply with just "I am a student, I need attachment."

**What To Do During Attachment:**
- Volunteer for every network job: New router install, Wi-Fi setup, cable termination, switch config
- Document everything: "Configured VLAN for Finance dept, 20 users, resolved DHCP issue"
- Get a reference letter that says WHAT you did, not just "he was here for 3 months"
- Take photos (with permission) of racks, configs (hide passwords) for your portfolio

**After Attachment:** You now have: DICT (in progress) + CCNA + 3 Months Real Experience. You are now more employable than many graduates.

## STAGE 4: AFTER DICT / YEAR 3 - GET SECURITY+ (Your Second Cert)
### Now You Add Security. Now You Become "Both Wins"

**Cert:** CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)
**Cost:** ~$439
**Study Time:** 2-3 months
**Why Now And Not Before?** You already have CCNA (building networks). Now you learn how to PROTECT them. This is what makes you a Network Security Engineer, not just a Network Engineer.

**What You Learn:** Threats, attacks, cryptography, firewalls, incident response, risk, compliance.

**Job After Security+:**
**SOC Analyst / Network Security Engineer / NOC Engineer**
**Pay in Kenya:** 80k - 150k KES

**Renewal Trick:** Security+ does NOT renew CCNA (different vendor - Cisco vs CompTIA). You will renew them separately:
- CCNA: Renew with CE credits or CCNP
- Security+: Renew with 50 CEUs + $150

Don't worry about this now. You have 3 years.

**Do You Still Need A+ and Network+ Now?**
**Optional, Not Required.**

After CCNA + Security+, you have two choices:
1. **Skip A+ and Network+ forever.** Your DICT + CCNA + Security+ proves you know more than A+ + Network+. Most employers will accept this. You saved $947.
2. **Get Network+ later if a specific job asks for it.** Some government / NGO jobs list Network+ specifically. If you see that job, you can get Network+ in 3 weeks because you already know it from CCNA. It will be easy for you.

**My advice for you:** Skip A+ and Network+ exams. Spend that money on CCNP later. Your time is better spent on CCNP than going backwards to easier certs.

## STAGE 5: YEAR 3 - 4 - CCNP ENTERPRISE (Become Senior)
### The Cert That Makes You A Senior Engineer

**Cert:** Cisco CCNP Enterprise
**Exams:** 2 exams - Core (350-401 ENCOR) + 1 Concentration (e.g., ENARSI)
**Cost:** ~$700+ total
**Study Time:** 6-12 months
**Prerequisite:** CCNA + Real experience (You will have both by now)

**What You Learn:** Design and run large enterprise networks. Advanced routing (BGP, OSPF), SD-WAN, automation, wireless, security.

**Job:** Senior Network Engineer / Network Architect
**Pay in Kenya:** 180k - 350k+ KES

**Why CCNP?** CCNA gets you the Network Engineer job. CCNP gets you the Senior job and the big salary. Without CCNP, you will stay at CCNA level for years.

## WHAT ABOUT A+ AND NETWORK+? - FINAL ANSWER FOR YOU

**Do NOT pay for A+ exam now.**
Your DICT Year 1 covers A+ knowledge. Your lecturers are teaching you hardware, OS, troubleshooting. Learn it well, but don't pay $548 to prove it.

**Do NOT pay for Network+ exam now.**
Your DICT networking + CCNA covers Network+ knowledge and MORE. CCNA is harder and worth more than Network+ for networking jobs.

**When WOULD you get Network+?**
Only if:
1. A specific job you want lists "Network+ required" and won't accept CCNA (rare), OR
2. You want an easy win later to renew / add to CV. After CCNA, Network+ will take you 2-3 weeks to pass because you already know 90% of it.

**Your money is better spent on:** CCNA discount voucher → Security+ → CCNP. Not going backwards.
