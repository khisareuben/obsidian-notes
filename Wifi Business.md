

**Starting a small WiFi hotspot business in Kenya typically requires about KSh 50,000–70,000 in startup capital. You’ll buy bulk internet from an ISP, set up routers and access points, integrate a billing system (like DiraLink or Mobilink), and then resell affordable packages to local users. Done right, you can recover your investment in 1–2 months.**

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup

### 1. **Internet Source**

- Start with **Safaricom Fibre, Faiba, Zuku, or Vipernet**.
    
- Entry package: **20–30 Mbps unlimited fibre** at ~KSh 3,000–7,000/month.
    
- This is your wholesale bandwidth to resell.
    

### 2. **Core Equipment**

- **MikroTik Router** – ~KSh 10,000 (handles hotspot login & billing).
    
- **Access Points (2–3 units)** – ~KSh 15,000 (extend coverage).
    
- **Network Switch** – ~KSh 5,000 (connects devices).
    
- **Cabling & Installation** – ~KSh 8,000.
    
- **Miscellaneous (mounts, UPS, etc.)** – ~KSh 2,000.
    

👉 **Total hardware: ~KSh 40,000.**

### 3. **Billing System**

- Start with **voucher-based login** (manual codes).
    
- Upgrade later to **M-Pesa integration** (automatic payments).
    
- Cost: ~KSh 5,000–10,000 depending on provider.
    

### 4. **Setup Process**

1. Configure MikroTik hotspot → captive portal login page.
    
2. Create user packages (e.g., KSh 10 = 2 hrs, KSh 20 = 12 hrs, KSh 30 = 24 hrs).
    
3. Limit speed per user (e.g., 2 Mbps) to prevent congestion.
    
4. Mount access points centrally for maximum coverage.
    

### 5. **Legal & Permissions**

- For small setups (apartment, estate, shop), you don’t need a CAK license.
    
- For scaling to multiple buildings or public hotspots, registration may be required.
    
- Always get landlord permission if installing in rental property.

## 💵 Revenue & Profit Projection

- **Users/day**: 25 (average spend KSh 20).
    
- **Monthly revenue**: 25 × 20 × 30 = **KSh 15,000**.
    
- **Expenses**: Internet ~KSh 7,000.
    
- **Profit**: ~KSh 8,000/month.
    
- With 40–60 users/day → **KSh 25,000–40,000 revenue**, profit ~KSh 15,000–30,000.
    

## ⚠️ Risks & Challenges

- **Slow speeds** if too many users connect → upgrade bandwidth.
    
- **Power outages** → small UPS backup recommended.
    
- **Competition** from Safaricom Fibre & Faiba.
    
- **Abuse** → throttle heavy downloaders.



## 🔑 How Local WiFi Billing Works

- **Hotspot setup** A router (often MikroTik) broadcasts WiFi. Users connect and are redirected to a **captive portal** (login/payment page).
    
- **Billing software** Platforms like DiraLink, Mobilink, NetiBora, or SPIDLAN automate payments, user authentication, and session management.
    
- **Payment integration** Customers pay via M-Pesa (Paybill, STK push, or vouchers). Once payment is confirmed, access is granted automatically.
    
- **Analytics & control** Owners can track usage, revenue, and customer behavior, and set limits (time-based or data-based packages).
