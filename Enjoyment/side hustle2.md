
## 📋 How It Works

- **Clients upload images or PDFs** (like receipts, invoices, or tables).
    
- **You manually type the data into Excel/Google Sheets** in a clean format.
    
- Sometimes you can use tools to speed up the process:
    
    - **Excel/Google Sheets OCR add‑ons** (convert scanned text to editable text).
        
    - **Python libraries** like `tabula-py` (extract tables from PDFs) or `pytesseract` (OCR for images).
